## Description

BatchBeacon AI connects product labels with official recall evidence so retailers can investigate affected stock and record reviewed responses. Gemini through Google ADK extracts structured label fields; deterministic rules check identity, lot, date and jurisdiction before a person confirms a demo inventory action. Cloud Run hosts the live app, Firebase/Cloud Firestore persists the owned investigation queue, and private Cloud Storage retains label evidence. Tested capture-guidance evaluation, activation and rollback change wording without weakening decisions. The prototype uses sample labels and historical SFA evidence; UK matching remains review-only. Planned work includes persistent accounts, a Consumer Recall Explorer, reviewed notice imagery and cross-country discovery. Permissioned, source-qualified trends could support consumers, producers and regulators; recall counts do not rank brand safety. A future pilot will measure review time and evidence completeness. No result grants food-safety clearance.
