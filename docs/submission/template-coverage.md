## Judging priorities

- **40% technical merit:** show actual image → Gemini/ADK proposals → deterministic
  rules → explicit human confirmation → persistent queue, with Google service roles.
- **25% retail alignment and impact:** explain the physical-package/notice comparison
  and handover problem; identify who benefits now and who could benefit later.
- **25% innovation:** demonstrate identity before batch, source-bound evidence,
  uncertainty/recovery and safe human responsibility. The integration is the
  proposed differentiation; competitor superiority is not established.
- **10% product experience:** show the optional five-step tutorial, example previews,
  English explanations, keyboard/touch help and phone queue. Pilot completion,
  assistance, repeat use and adoption remain unmeasured.

Future discovery must retain country, notice date, affected identity/batch and source
coverage. Recall counts do not establish brand safety, disease causation or global
completeness. The current release has one reviewed historical SFA scope; UK matching
remains review-only under U17. Consumer/authority dashboards and persistent accounts
are future work, not hidden completed features.

Final artifact review still requires exact PDF size/readability/link checks and
public video playback. Form submission and acceptance of current material terms
require the separate U16 OWNER-SUBMIT gate and an actual receipt.
