improvement.

## Decision and remaining gates

**The compact input is not selected for latency enablement.** Original input is restored
at 100% traffic through a newly reviewed conditional transition on the same compatible
image. Final readback verifies runtime 901, original admission settings, MEDIUM/24,576,
min0, unchanged resources/IAM and all 491 records. Six genuine current-owner GETs verify
original records, successful derived records and the financially reconciled failed
derived record under that reader, with no new model or guest request. Every original
expiry, history entry, charge and older unknown hold is preserved. The stage measurements,
loading fixes and heartbeat correction remain shipped.

Reviewed-source interpretation reuse is deferred because it changes per-call agreement,
review/currentness and invalidation contracts; technical source completeness is not
human source/gold custody. Provider caching adds untested TTL/privacy/accounting
boundaries. Model/concurrency alternatives have no demonstrated benefit under this
experiment and remain deferred. The owner excludes lower reasoning and warming;
no indirect workaround or keepalive was introduced.

Human automatic VoiceOver announcements/recovery, unfamiliar-user comprehension,
source/gold review and protected custody/quality, wider Operations/cleanup/video gates
remain separate and open. UK remains review-only. No safety, contamination, sale-release,
worldwide-completeness or general model-accuracy claim follows from these observations.
