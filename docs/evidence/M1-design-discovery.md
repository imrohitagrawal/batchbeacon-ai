# Replacement design discovery

Status: “Evidence in focus” structure selected; color/identity revision in progress;
concrete desktop/phone visual approval remains open.
This is preparation, not final DESIGN or evidence of a working product.

## Confirmed brief

Retail staff need to check an exact product against reviewed official recall notices,
understand missing or outdated evidence and deliberately record a simulated next
action. Workflow A remains accepted. The first replacement's old appearance,
generic dashboard composition, unclear purpose and color combination were rejected.
The owner wants a contemporary enterprise finish, soothing deliberate color and
attention earned through composition. Detailed direction and the first 30 seconds
are delegated to the designer; concrete visual approval is still required.

The first 30 seconds should expose the product's mechanism: understand the exact
check, choose a labelled synthetic example or capture a label, inspect identity and
batch/date evidence alongside the official notice, then see the appropriate human
decision. An incomplete or stale case visibly changes that path. This supports the
accepted technical/impact/innovation/UX rubric by making product behavior legible.

Read-only visual references inspected in the browser:

- [Apple](https://www.apple.com/in/): focus, hierarchy, disciplined whitespace and
  a small number of clear actions. Adapt the precision to a working screen.
- [Cleartrip](https://www.cleartrip.com/): prominent task inputs and an unmistakable
  action. Keep operational clarity without promotional density.
- [MakeMyTrip](https://www.makemytrip.com/): clear service categories and separation
  of navigation from the task surface; the main form was only partially loaded in
  the inspected capture, so no detailed form conclusion is claimed.
- [Google Design](https://design.google/): confident type, organized contrast and
  color applied to meaningful regions. This is a visual-quality reference, not a
  choice to copy its editorial page or Google's identity.

Reference captures remain private, outside shipping assets. No logos, photography,
commercial claims or proprietary brand assets are being copied.

## Material prepared before the concept roll

Mechanism: source-supported label extraction feeds a deterministic, identity-first
recall comparison and a deliberately confirmed simulated action.
Scene: a retailer checks packaging at a bright counter, or a judge tries the same
bounded workflow on a laptop/phone. Mode: Operate.
Avoid both the category default (dark rail, summary cards, KPI grid) and its obvious
opposite (oversized marketing headline with the task pushed below the fold).

Seven grounded systems, ordered before the roll. Each may lend type, palette,
density and one signature move; standard web controls and the task own navigation.

| Position | Grounded system | Resonance and visual contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | International public-information typography | Fast comprehension across markets; cool blue field, white working area, charcoal sans, one clear evidence progression with the current decision emphasized. |
| 2 | Consumer-electronics specification sheets | Exact identity and comparable attributes; silver-white, graphite and sapphire, precise sans, aligned label/notice values that highlight together. |
| 3 | Travel itinerary confirmations | A clear beginning, supporting detail and deliberate next action; pale sky, navy and warm semantic warning, compact sans, source/version changes shown in an orderly history strip. |
| 4 | Contemporary grocery packaging systems | Product-first recognition at a glance; pale mineral green, deep blue and white, generous sans, the selected synthetic product stays visually anchored during review. |
| 5 | Museum collection labels | Object and provenance remain adjacent; mist-gray, ink and muted cornflower, restrained type scale, selected label regions reveal their source-linked field. |
| 6 | Modern statistical publication graphics | Explicit distinctions and honest uncertainty; white, slate, azure and amber, tabular alignment, matched/unknown/excluded evidence carries words and consistent marks. |
| 7 | Color-indexed reference-book editions | Many related records remain findable; pale blue-gray, aubergine ink and sea-glass accents, compact sans, source families retain consistent navigation marks across the queue and review. |

The executable roll, assigned direction, challenger judgments and actual visual
options will be recorded separately after bounded skill admission. None of these
rows is an owner-approved visual direction.

## Actual Impeccable discovery, mapped to this project

The unchanged project-local Impeccable 4.6.0 was loaded and its admitted engine
context/real concept roll/interactive workshop were run. The earlier prototype
used an adapted reference subset; it did not complete this original workflow.
Impeccable is guidance executed by the design agent, not a separate designer with
hidden answers or a fixed long questionnaire.

Its [init](../../.agents/skills/impeccable/reference/init.md) and
[shape](../../.agents/skills/impeccable/reference/shape.md) prompts cover:

| Discovery question (condensed from the skill) | Answer/evidence in this project |
| --- | --- |
| Who uses this, in what situation, for which job? | Retail staff checking packaging; laptop review and phone capture; a judge can try synthetic examples. Bright counter is a design assumption, not an observed research finding. |
| What does it make possible, and what is distinctive? | Label/source comparison with identity before lot, explicit uncertainty and human-confirmed simulated actions. |
| What must be preserved? | Approved workflows, real reviewed source facts, exact identifiers, synthetic labels, bounded scope and human gates. |
| What should someone understand or do first? | Understand “check this product against selected official recalls”; choose a sample, inspect the comparison, decide deliberately. Detailed first-30-second design delegated by owner. |
| What content, ranges and assets must it carry? | Long product names, typed codes, date rules, original/update history, three reviewed SFA labels, six illustrative queue records. UK human review remains pending. |
| Which states and transitions matter? | First use, capture, unreadable panel, match, identity mismatch, source change, unavailable check, review confirmation, queue empty/filter and guidance evaluation/confirmation/rollback. |
| What fidelity and scope? | Interactive desktop/phone prototype for concrete visual review, then integrated product after approval. |
| What would make it wrong despite polish? | Generic AI dashboard, old finish, unclear purpose, default blue-gray/green-beige palettes, weak selection boundaries, generic circle logo. |
| Which accessibility/delivery constraints bind? | NF-06: keyboard-complete, labels, focus, announced changes/errors, non-color outcomes, contrast, reduced motion, 390px phone and crucial identifiers at 200% zoom. |

The skill says to ask only material gaps and avoid repeating settled facts. The
owner has answered the discovery round and delegated detailed judgment; no further
palette questionnaire is needed to prepare a reviewable revision.

## Workshop outcome and refinement brief

Real roll assigned world 6, seed reference `seed:42aa328248450bcd`; the complete
workshop rendered three primary studies and a standard comparator. The owner
selected **Evidence in focus**, then rejected the palette. This accepts the adjacent
label / evidence / decision structure, not final visuals, compliance or functionality.

Earlier proposed refinement (superseded by the palette amendment below): clean white work surfaces, charcoal type, limited clear orange
for brand/action, and warm amber for review conditions. No cream/serif treatment,
dark rail or blue-gray panel wash. Selection is a native checked control plus full
boundary and explicit text. The precise orange remains a designer proposal.

Proposed identity: two offset label forms that share an alignment edge and suggest
a B. Packaging evidence meets an authority record. It must work in one color and at
16/24/32px. No tick/shield that implies safety approval; no copied reference brand.

## Research translated into checks

Primary guidance inspected: [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/),
[target sizing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html),
[reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html),
[native radio interaction](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/radio/), and
GOV.UK patterns for [starting a service](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/start-using-a-service/)
and [single-choice inputs](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/radios/).
The proposed implementation uses an immediately visible task, short preparatory
copy, native controls, explicit selection labels, and actionable recovery. This is
a design inference from guidance, not evidence of adoption by actual retailers.

Prototype checks: computed text/control contrast, keyboard choice/confirmation,
focus return, semantic names/status, 390px and 320px reflow, equivalent 200% layout,
reduced motion, source/incident binding and explanatory unavailable states. Target
44px controls as a design choice above WCAG 2.2 AA's 24px minimum/spacing exceptions.
Full assistive-technology and deployed availability evidence remain product gates.
