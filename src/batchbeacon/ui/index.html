Who benefits and what comes next

Available today: the retailer workbench compares a sample label with a reviewed historical notice; the retail queue keeps reviewed investigations in this guest shop. Private technical Operations is separate from this public interface. Inventory actions are for demonstration.

Planned consumer dashboard: discover recalls by product, country and officially reported hazard; inspect affected batches, dates and source links. Planned Google/email accounts would retain owned investigations across sessions.

Wider impact we are working toward: consumers and the public can find affected batches and official advice; retailers and producers can coordinate traceable responses; regulators and monitoring teams can examine recurring reported issues. Cross-border discovery would show notices from relevant countries while preserving each notice’s jurisdiction and product scope. Cross-country sources, notice imagery and aggregate trends require further review and implementation. Adoption and population-level impact have not yet been measured.

Recall counts alone do not rank brand safety. Health risks must be attributed to official notices; absence from a notice does not establish safety.

Read the one-minute walkthrough
  1. Choose and load a label. Select an example, keep the reviewed SFA source selected, then choose Use this label. The label image was created for this prototype; the linked notice is an actual historical recall publication.
  2. Extract and compare. Choose Extract and check once the image is retained. This uses the available Gemini allowance. AI proposes the text; server rules check product identity before lot, date and jurisdiction. Click a field to inspect its evidence.
  3. Review the result. A match can support a simulated hold. Missing or uncertain evidence needs review or another readable capture. “Not listed” never means safe to consume or sell. Add a capture retains another synthetic image; Correct a field preserves history and requires re-verification.
  4. Confirm and find your work. When available, choose Review simulated hold or Review simulated review request, inspect the evidence and tick the confirmation before saving. Open Retail queue to filter, refresh or reopen your investigation. It stays in this browser’s bounded guest shop until access expires.
  5. Improve capture guidance. Open Capture guidance when an incident needs a clearer label capture. A complete matched label may have no eligible guidance request. For eligible evidence, request guidance, record structured feedback, propose a wording change and run its fixed evaluation. A passing evaluation still needs explicit activation; rollback restores wording at a newer revision. Guidance changes do not change recall facts or verdicts.

If a control is disabled, read the nearby status. An interrupted operation must be reconciled with its original request before retrying. This tour describes the workflow; it does not create results or spend model allowance.
